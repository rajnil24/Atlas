**Biography Reports**

### Introduction

This report presents biographical information on two prominent technology industry leaders: Sundar Pichai and Tim Cook. The report provides an overview of their careers, achievements, and current roles within their respective companies.

### Sundar Pichai Biography

Sundar Pichai is the CEO of Google and Alphabet, serving on Alphabet's Board of Directors. He joined Google in 2004 and played a key role in the development of Google Toolbar and Google Chrome, which became the world's most popular internet browser. In 2014, Pichai was appointed to lead product and engineering for all of Google's products and platforms, including Search, Maps, Play, Android, Chrome, Gmail, and Google Apps (now G Suite). He became Google's CEO in August 2015 and joined the Board of Directors of Alphabet in July 2017.

Under Pichai's leadership, Google has invested in new opportunities such as Google Cloud and YouTube, and has continued to be a leader in advanced technologies, including machine learning and quantum computing. In December 2019, Pichai took on the additional role of CEO of Alphabet, Google's parent company.

### Tim Cook Biography

Tim Cook is an American business executive who has served as the CEO of Apple since 2011. Born on November 1, 1960, Cook was appointed chief executive of Apple on August 24, 2011, after Steve Jobs resigned. During his tenure, Cook has advocated for political reform of international and domestic surveillance, cybersecurity, national manufacturing, and environmental preservation.

Under Cook's leadership, Apple has experienced significant growth, with revenue and profit doubling between 2011 and 2020. The company's market value has also increased dramatically, from $348 billion to $1.9 trillion. In 2014, Cook made history by becoming the first and only chief executive of a Fortune 500 company to publicly come out as gay.

### Conclusion

Sundar Pichai and Tim Cook are two highly influential figures in the technology industry, with impressive track records of leadership and innovation. Their biographies offer valuable insights into their careers, achievements, and contributions to their respective companies.
