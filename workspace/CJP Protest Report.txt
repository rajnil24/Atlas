**Introduction**

This report provides an overview of the Cockroach Janta Party protest that took place in Delhi. The protest was held to demand the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan over the NEET paper leak issue.

**Protest Details**

The protest was organized by the Cockroach Janta Party, a social media platform, and was held at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi. The Delhi Police granted permission for the protest to take place on Saturday morning. The founder of the Cockroach Janta Party, Abhijeet Dipke, traveled from the US to lead the protest.

**Purpose of the Protest**

The primary objective of the protest was to demand accountability, seek justice, and call for the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The protest was sparked by the NEET paper leak issue, which has been a subject of controversy in recent times. The protesters sought to emphasize the need for the minister's resignation in light of the leak.

**Notable Participants**

The protest was joined by notable figures, including activist and innovator Sonam Wangchuk. Wangchuk's participation added significant weight to the protest, highlighting the importance of the issue and the need for action.

**Protest Activities**

During the protest, a pamphlet was distributed to participants, outlining the purpose and objectives of the demonstration. The pamphlet emphasized the demand for accountability, justice, and the resignation of the education minister.

**Conclusion**

The Cockroach Janta Party protest in Delhi was a significant event that highlighted the need for accountability and justice in the wake of the NEET paper leak issue. The protest, which was joined by notable figures such as Sonam Wangchuk, demonstrated the public's demand for action and the need for the education minister's resignation.
